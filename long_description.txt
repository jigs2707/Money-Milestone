Money Milestone is a savings goal tracker that turns "I should really start saving" into money actually set aside. Pick a goal, give it a target and a date, and watch every deposit move the bar.

No bank logins. No spreadsheets. No subscription. Just a clear savings plan you can stick to.


SET SAVINGS GOALS THAT ACTUALLY GET FINISHED

Create as many savings goals as you like — a holiday, a car, an emergency fund, a wedding, a deposit on your first home. Each goal gets a name, a target amount, a deadline and a category.

Add a deposit whenever you put money aside. Need some back? Log a withdrawal. Every transaction is saved against the goal, so you always know exactly where the money went and how much is left to save.


SEE EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE

The savings dashboard adds up every goal: total saved, total target and your overall progress across the board. Open any goal for a progress ring, a savings chart that plots your deposits over time, days remaining, and the full transaction history.

Filter goals by All, Pending or Completed. Filter by category. Sort by progress, by days remaining or by amount saved. Find the goal you care about in a tap.


BUILD A SAVING STREAK

Save something — anything — each day and your streak grows. Your current streak and your personal best sit on the home screen as a daily nudge. Small amounts, repeated, are what actually build savings.


REMINDERS THAT KEEP YOU GOING

Money Milestone nudges you at the moments that matter, and every reminder can be switched off or rescheduled:

• Daily savings reminder at a time you choose
• Streak reminder before the day runs out
• Morning motivation to start the day right
• Weekly progress report every Sunday
• Deadline alerts 7, 3 and 1 day before a goal is due
• Milestone alerts at 25%, 50%, 75% and 90%
• A celebration the moment a goal is fully funded


UNLOCK 16 BADGES

Saving is easier when it feels like a game. Earn badges for your first goal, your first 50%, finishing a goal early, completing five goals and more — each with a full-screen celebration the first time you unlock it.


MAKE IT YOURS

SAVED AND SYNCED

Your goals and transactions are stored securely in the cloud, so they are backed up and waiting for you on any device you sign in to.

Anyone who has tried a budgeting app and bounced off it because tracking every coffee is exhausting — Money Milestone only asks you to log what you save.

Money Milestone: Piggy Bank — Savings Goal Tracker

Turn your financial dreams into real results. Money Milestone: Piggy Bank helps you set savings goals, track your progress, and stay motivated every step of the way — all in one beautifully designed app.

Whether you're saving for a vacation, a new car, an emergency fund, or your first home — Money Milestone: Piggy Bank keeps you focused, organised, and celebrating every win.


SET GOALS. TRACK PROGRESS. ACHIEVE MORE.

Create as many savings goals as you need. Give each one a name, a target amount, a deadline, and a category. Then watch your progress bar grow with every deposit you make. Add deposits and withdrawals at any time, see exactly how much you've saved, and view every transaction you've made for each goal. A live savings dashboard shows your total saved, total target, and overall progress across all goals at a glance.


STAY ORGANISED YOUR WAY

Choose from built-in categories like Travel, Home, Car, Education, and Emergency Fund — or create your own. Pick a name, choose from 30+ icons, and select a colour to make it uniquely yours.

Quickly find what you're looking for with smart filters and sorting. Filter goals by status — All, Pending, or Completed. Filter by category, or sort by progress, remaining days, or amount saved.


NEVER LOSE MOMENTUM

Money Milestone: Piggy Bank keeps you on track with intelligent, fully local notifications. Your data never leaves your device.

Daily Savings Reminder sends a gentle nudge to make a deposit every day. Streak Reminder makes sure you don't break your savings streak. Morning Motivation starts every day with an inspiring boost at 8 AM. Weekly Progress Report gives you a Sunday summary of how your week went. Deadline Alerts warn you 7, 3, and 1 day before any goal's due date. Milestone Alerts celebrate every time you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% of a goal. Goal Completion sends a special notification the moment you fully fund a goal.

All notification types are individually toggleable and you can set your own reminder times from the Notifications screen.


BUILD YOUR SAVINGS STREAK

Deposit even once a day and your streak grows. Your current and longest streaks are always visible on the home screen — a small daily habit that compounds into big results.


UNLOCK BADGES AND ACHIEVEMENTS

Saving money is more fun when there's something to celebrate. Money Milestone: Piggy Bank has 17 badges across four categories — from taking your first step to completing five goals.

Each badge unlocks with a full-screen celebration the first time you earn it. Watch your badge shelf fill up as your savings habit grows. Badges include First Step for creating your very first goal, Halfway There for reaching 50% on any goal, Achiever for completing your first goal, Early Bird for finishing a goal before its deadline, Grand Slam for completing 5 or more goals, and many more.


BEAUTIFUL DESIGN. DARK MODE INCLUDED.

Money Milestone: Piggy Bank is built with a premium, modern design that feels great in both light and dark mode. Switch themes any time from your profile settings — your preference is saved automatically.


YOUR CURRENCY. YOUR NUMBERS.

Pick any currency from a searchable list of global currencies. The symbol and code you choose appear everywhere amounts are displayed, so the app always feels local to you.


SECURE AND PRIVATE

Your goals and transactions are securely stored in the cloud so they're always backed up and available across devices. All notifications are processed entirely on-device — no notification data is ever sent to any server.


HOW TO GET STARTED

Create a free account with your email, add your first savings goal, make your first deposit, and watch your progress grow. It really is that simple.

Money Milestone: Piggy Bank is free to download and use. Start saving smarter today.
